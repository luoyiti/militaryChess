# 南翼研究日志（south 代理）

范围：1941-06-22 → 1941-12-05，德国南方集团军群（第 6、17、11 集团军，第 1 装甲集群）、罗马尼亚第 3/4 集团军、匈牙利喀尔巴阡集群/快速军、意大利 CSIR、斯洛伐克远征军；苏联西南方面军、南方面军、滨海独立集团军、第 51 集团军、第 56 集团军、黑海舰队。
时间一律为柏林时间（CEST）；莫斯科时间 = 柏林 + 1 h。

## 1. Dubno–Lutsk–Brody tank battle (23–30 June)
Source: ru.wikipedia «Сражение в районе Дубно — Луцк — Броды»; en.wikipedia Battle of Brody (1941).
- 23.6: 15 MK (10 TD) meets 11. PzD at Radekhov; Germans take Styr crossings at Berestechko.
- 24.6: 22 MK (19 TD, 215 MD) attacks from Voinitsa line N of Vladimir-Volynsky–Lutsk road, fails; Kondrusev killed. 19 MK reaches Ikva at Mlynov.
- 25.6: 11. PzD takes Dubno; 8 MK ends ~500 km march near Brody, loses up to 50% vehicles.
- 26.6: 13. PzD enters Lutsk (morning). 43 TD reaches Dubno outskirts ~18:00, pulls back W of Rovno at night. 8 MK enters Brody, attacks 8–15 km toward Berestechko.
- 27.6: Popel group (34 TD + 24 TR) breaks into Dubno area from the south (14:00 attack, evening at Dubno outskirts). Rokossovsky (9 MK) goes over to defence at Klevan/Malyn.
- 27/28.6: 16. ID(mot), 75., 111. ID close behind Popel; 28.6 Vashugin suicide.
- 29.6: 15 MK withdraws to Zolochiv heights; Germans take Brody, Busk. 8 MK fights out to Zolochiv.
- 1.7: 8 MK at Tarnopol with 207 tanks. Popel group breaks out ~1–2 July (34 TD commander Col. I. V. Vasiliev killed).

## 3. Hungarian Mobile Corps (Andaházi Szeghy Viktor PhD thesis, NKE 2009, ch. II; en.wikipedia Gyorshadtest)
- 1.7 Carpathian Group (Szombathelyi; VIII Corps 1st Mtn & 8th Border Bdes + Mobile Corps) attacks over the Carpathians; 3–4.7 Miklós group via Kolomyja and Gorodenka to the Dniester; 7.7 Dniester reached; 9.7 Mobile Corps under German command (Carpathian Group dissolved).
- 11.7 1st Cav Bde crosses the Dniester (Mihalcea bridge) and old border at Skala; 13.7 ordered to assemble at Kamenets-Podolsky; 19.7 2nd Mot Bde attacks toward Bar; 21.7 Zhmerinka (two-day battle); 23.7 Tulchin; 24.7 Tulchin–Bratslav line broken.
- 3.8 lead of the mot. brigade reaches crossroads 3 km W of Pervomaisk (link with 16. PzD; Uman pocket closed).
- 10–16.8 Battle of Nikolaev: two mot. bdes fight between Bug and Ingul on a 30–40 km front, enter Nikolaev with the Germans.
- 19–29.8 pause; by 28.8 assembled at Krivoi Rog.
- 30.8–6.10 Dnieper river defence in the Zaporozhye bend (Nikopol–Zaporozhye; after Khortitsa island evacuated, a 55 km sector opposite Zaporozhye).
- 10–28.10 advance to the Donets under Gruppe Schwedler (IV AK): via Dnepropetrovsk, Mikhailovka, Nadezhdovka, Sokolje; mud; 24–26.10 Petropole–Savodsk line; Izium area cleared 5.11.
- 6–12.11 relieved by German 257. ID; 15.11 withdrawn; assemble at Dnepropetrovsk by 23.11; foot elements to Vinnitsa from 26.11; leave Vinnitsa 2–4.12; home via Kolomyja–Kőrösmező (Uzhgorod by 12.12).
- Strength ~25,000 (75–80 %), 65 CV-35 tankettes, 95 Toldi I. Losses 1941: >2,700 dead, >1,500 missing, ≥7,500 wounded; 80 % of Toldis, all CV-35s lost.
- Kamenets-Podolsky massacre 27–28.8.1941: Jews deported by Hungary murdered by Jeckeln's SS/police (~23,600 victims).

## 6. Romanian forces (Axworthy; ro/ru/en wiki Odessa)
- Mountain Corps (1, 2, 4 Mtn Bdes): N. Bukovina early July (Cernăuți 5.7), Dniester crossing 17.7 (1st & 4th lead), Bug, Dnieper, Nogai steppe (late Sept, 4th Bde pushed back), Azov, Crimea (1st & 4th stay; 2nd home in Nov, 1,926 casualties).
- 4th Army Odessa: 3rd Corps on Razdelnaya–Odessa axis (W/SW: Dalnik), 5th Corps (15 ID, 1st Cav Bde, +1st Armd Div night 5/6.8) crossed Dniester between Tighina and Dubăsari, swung N via Katargy then S; 8.8 siege regime; 10.8 general attack; 13.8 encirclement by land complete; 20.8 renewed assault halted 8–15 km out; 9.9 Iacobici replaces Ciupercă; 16–17.9 157 SD lands; 22.9 Grigorievka landing (3rd Marine Rgt ~1,929 men) + 157/421 SD defeat RO 13 & 15 ID; 30.9 Stavka orders evacuation; 2–4.10 Soviet local attack; 5.10 Petrov replaces Sofronov; 1–16.10 evacuation (86,000 troops, 15,000 civilians, 462 guns); last ship 05:30 16.10; RO losses ~92,545 (17,729 dead).
- Odessa massacre 22–24.10 after the NKVD-building bomb (Gen. Glogojanu killed): est. 25,000–34,000 Jews killed (Dalnik etc.).

## 9. Discrepancies with fronts keyframes (for the fronts agent / integrator)
- Nogai steppe 15.9–4.10: keyframes put the Axis line near 33.7°E (Kakhovka bridgehead) at lat 46.85; historically 11. Armee/RO 3rd Army stood on the Molochnaya line NW of Melitopol (~34.8–35.3°E) when the Soviet 9/18 A counteroffensive hit on 26.9 (136 SD at Malaya Belozerka 28.9). My units (XXX AK, LSSAH, XLIX, RO 3A) are 40–80 km east of the drawn line.
- Lutsk–Kovel 24.6–4.7: keyframes cover Kovel/Rozhishche/Kivertsy (lat 50.8–51.3, to ~26.0°E) earlier than my 5th Army units (15 SK, 27 SK, 22 MK, 62/87 SD) — ~30–40 km.
- Kerch 15.11: keyframe still Soviet around Kerch while 46/73/170 ID are at its outskirts (Kerch fell 16.11).
- Ishun 22–24.10 and NE Romania/Hungary before the July offensives: Axis units at home or at the start line appear "outside" (expected).
